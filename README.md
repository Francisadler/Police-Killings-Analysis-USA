This is an Analysis of the deaths involving the police of the United states from the beginning of 2015 to mid 2017 (2.5 years).
The Analysis involves numerous charts and graphs that illustrate the relationship between various factors such as race, gender, city, mental state of the victims and the killings by the police.
The analysis incoporates bar charts, histograms, pie charts, box plots, kernel density estimation, regression graphs, line graphs and choropleths to reveal various insights in the given data.
